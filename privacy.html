Privacy policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Lowie is a private beta. This page explains what we collect on this website and why. It is short because we collect very little.

Who is responsible

Lowie, a project being set up in France. A contact address will be published on this page before the beta opens.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you join the waitlist.
  • The link you came from, if it carries a ?ref= tag (for example, the post that brought you).
  • Your answer to "What are you building?", only if you choose to send it.

We count visits with Vercel Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and does not identify you. We use no advertising trackers and no cookies.

Why

To email you once when your beta spot opens, and to count which posts bring people to Lowie. We never sell or share your email.

Where it is stored

The waitlist is stored with Supabase in the EU (Paris region). The website is hosted by Vercel. Fonts are served from this website, not from a third party.

How long

Until the beta opens and you have been invited, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time, using the contact address above once it is published. You can also complain to the CNIL (cnil.fr).
